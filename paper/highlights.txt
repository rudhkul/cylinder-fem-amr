Highlights (Computers & Mathematics with Applications; 3-5 bullets, max. 85 characters each)

- Remesh force spikes are set by the re-projection operator, not the BDF2 history
- Stabilised re-projection avoids the 300-400x drag spikes of an unstabilised one
- This re-projection is near the identity on an unchanged mesh, but not exactly
- Keeping the stabilised divergence in the rotational term grows the pressure error
- Adaptivity lowers the error per node at the coarse end but not the GPU wall time
